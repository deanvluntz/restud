# Sources of the 2017–19 extensions of the Millennium series (`ons_extension_2017_2019.csv`, `boe_a47_cpi_1955_2019.csv`)

Every Millennium (Thomas and Dimsdale, 2017, v3.1) series used in the manuscript ends in 2016. The values for 2017–19 are
spliced onto the 2016 Millennium level from the current official source below (annual averages of monthly/quarterly data
unless stated). Columns of `ons_extension_2017_2019.csv`:

| Column | 2017–19 source | Series identifier |
|---|---|---|
| `vacancies_k` | ONS Vacancy Survey, annual average | AP2Y |
| `unemployed_k_a50`, `vu_ratio` | ONS Labour Force Survey unemployment level, 16+ | MGSC (ratio = AP2Y / MGSC) |
| `unem_spliced_boe`, `unem_lfs_a1` | ONS LFS unemployment rate, 16+ | MGSX |
| `wage_inflation` | ONS average weekly earnings, regular pay, annual growth | KAB9 |
| `tbill_rate` | Bank of England Interactive Database: Treasury bill tender rate to June 2017; Bank Rate thereafter less the Jan 2016–Jun 2017 mean gap (−0.1075) | IUMAAJNB, IUMABEDR |
| `real_eri` | BIS effective exchange rate indices, real narrow index, UK: growth applied to the Millennium 2016 level | BIS EER (real, narrow, GB) |
| `house_price_index` | HM Land Registry UK House Price Index: growth applied to the Millennium 2016 level | UK HPI, all dwellings |
| `debt_interest_tme` | ONS Public Sector Finances (PUSF, July 2026): public sector interest and dividends paid (ANLO) over total managed expenditure (EBFT), the definition the Millennium sheet names (Sam, 29 Sep 2026); growth since 2016 applied to the Millennium 2016 level (replaces the OBR HPFD splice of 27 Sep and the NMFX construction tried earlier the same day, which is kept in the file as `int_tme_nmfx`). The full ANLO/EBFT ratio 1946–2025 is carried as `int_tme_ons` | ANLO, EBFT (`ons_pusf_debt_interest_1946_2025.csv`, from `ons_pusf_jul2026.xlsx`) |
| `ca_gdp` | ONS Balance of Payments (dataset PNBP): current account balance as % of GDP, used IN FULL for every year from 29 Sep 2026 (Sam: use the ONS series rather than splicing); the Millennium-plus-splice version is kept as `ca_gdp_mill` (correlation 0.98 with AA6H over 1962–2016, mean gap 0.24 points, largest 0.84 in 2016) | AA6H (`ons_AA6H_current_account_gdp_1948_2025.xls`, Sam, 29 Sep 2026) |
| `trade_bal_gdp` | ONS UK trade tables (July 2026), Table 3A: total trade in goods and services, balance, excluding precious metals, £m, divided by OBR nominal GDP; change since 2016 applied to the Millennium 2016 level | FSID (`tradepublicationtables2026jul.xlsx`, Sam, 29 Sep 2026); GDP from `fiscal_block_HPFD.csv` |
| `terms_of_trade` | ONS UK trade tables (July 2026), Table 13: implied deflators of total trade in goods and services excluding precious metals, exports over imports; growth since 2016 applied to the Millennium 2016 level. ONS YBGJ (terms of trade effect, SA CVM, £m, from 1997) is the national-accounts trading gain, a level in £m, not a price ratio, and is not used | JIO7 / JIO6 |
| `labour_share` | ONS UK Economic Accounts: compensation of employees as % of GDP, current prices, SA, used IN FULL for every year from 29 Sep 2026; the Millennium-plus-splice version is kept as `labour_share_mill`. The two are different concepts (Millennium ~70–78%, at factor cost with self-employment income; IHXP 45–60%, employees only, at market prices) and correlate only 0.40 over 1962–2016, so results using the labour share change with the switch | IHXP (`ons_IHXP_compensation_employees_gdp_1948_2025.xls`, Sam, 29 Sep 2026) |
| `cpi_infl` (separate file) | ONS CPI annual average inflation | D7G7 (2017 2.7, 2018 2.5, 2019 1.8; confirmed by Sam 28 Sep 2026) |

Manuscript references: ONS (2026); Bank of England (2026); Bank for International Settlements (2026); HM Land Registry (2026);
OBR (2026a). Columns that use an extended series are re-estimated to 2016 under each panel ("proj, to 2016").

The debt-interest ratio is in Sam's core specification. On the Millennium definition (ANLO/EBFT) the current PUSF vintage gives the same
result as the Millennium series (core specification: 0.146 p .03 extended, 0.156 p .007 core, best AIC of the three; Millennium 0.124 /
0.129); the NMFX net-interest construction gives a null term and was a different object. Both ANLO/EBFT versions are carried (`int_tme`
Millennium-based with the 2017–19 growth splice, `int_tme_ons` PUSF for every year) and the parallel battery reports the core specification under each.
The labour-market, earnings, bill-rate, exchange-rate and house-price splices (AP2Y, MGSC, MGSX, KAB9, IUMAAJNB, BIS EER, UK HPI) could not be
re-audited in this session because the container has no access to the ONS, Bank or BIS sites; the LFS and AWE series are the same ONS
sources the Millennium tables reproduce, and the Vacancy Survey (AP2Y) is the Millennium M5 source from 2001, so those splices are
level-consistent by construction. Sam can send the downloads if an explicit overlap check is wanted.
